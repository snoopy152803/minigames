Puzzle Admin

Edits here change the puzzle for everyone, stylesheets in GitHub shared/admin.css or shared/style.css.

Wordle — answer words

One 5-letter word per line. This is the pool the daily/random answer is picked from (the much larger accepted-guess dictionary is not admin-editable — it lives in data/wordle/guesses/).

Strands — puzzles

Each puzzle needs a theme name, a spangram (the long word/phrase spanning the board), and a handful of other themed words. Plain words only — no spaces or punctuation. The board layout is generated automatically.

Crossword — clue database

A word plus its clue. Include some long/hard words (they anchor the grid) and some short ones (they fill in intersections).
